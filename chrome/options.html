When on, overlays are removed as soon as any page loads or changes, on every site, with no click needed. When off, click the toolbar button (or press Ctrl+Shift+X) to remove overlays manually, as before.

Sites to skip

One hostname per line. Auto-remove is skipped on these sites, useful for apps whose own overlays (like a cart or address picker) look like a popup. Tip: right-click the toolbar icon and choose "Toggle auto-remove for this site" to add/remove the current site instantly.
